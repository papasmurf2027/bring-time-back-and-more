Rules — short version

  1. Owner is not responsible for refunds. Refunds only within 12 hours of receipt, for a legit reason only (not as described, damaged, wrong item, defective, never arrived). "Changed my mind" is not accepted.
  2. Shipping must be insured. Buyers pay shipping — the price is set by package size: Small $4.47, Medium $4.85, Large $5.25, XL $6.75, Box 1–5 lb $7.75, Big box over 5 lb $9.69+, Flat Rate box $12.99, Card envelope $0.99. Carrier is automatic: everything ships via USPS, except Big box over 5 lb, which ships via UPS. No FedEx.
  3. Winner's payment is checked first (test check only — no real processor yet) and held in escrow. Seller is paid only after delivery + 12 hours.
  4. To ask for a refund you must send BOTH a photo AND a video of the item. Winners get a receipt + video receipt as proof.
  5. Replicas / unbranded items must say so. A replica cannot be claimed as real. Banned items are blocked. Report a listing if something is wrong.
  6. Auctions: bid slider, custom bid, 10s / 30s / 2 min / custom timer. Sudden Death = outbid does not reset the timer. Regular = outbid adds 10 seconds.
  7. Store is Buy Now only — no bidding, no timer. No automatic Buy It Now in auctions; the seller may post a Buy It Now offer in chat.
  8. Founding sellers: first 10, 0% until it goes live, then 5% forever. Normal fee is 10% on the item price only, not shipping.
  9. Live Now means live on camera + chat — watch the seller and hear them talk, and type in chat what you want to see.
  10. When someone buys or wins and pays, the seller gets a QR code for that exact sale. The QR names the carrier — USPS, or UPS for a Big box over 5 lb. Scan it to open the sale, then tap Mark as shipped. The QR is a shipment record — not real postage yet.
  11. ⏰ Time Wheel: the seller loads the wheel with up to 100 items and buyers see them all before bidding. The winner pays the winning bid + shipping, the wheel spins and lands on one item — that item is what they get. No extra prize, no extra charge. Nobody buys a spot and nobody pays to spin.
  12. 🎲 Random Items: if a seller doesn't want to put a picture up, they can auction numbered random items — Random Item 1, 2, 3, up to 100 — with no picture. The seller holds each item up live on camera. What the winner gets is what was shown live, and the seller types what it was on the sold item page — the video receipt then shows what they won.
