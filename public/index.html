Rules — short version

  1. Owner is not responsible for refunds. Refunds only within 12 hours of receipt, for a legit reason only (not as described, damaged, wrong item, defective, never arrived). "Changed my mind" is not accepted.
  2. Shipping must be insured. Buyers pay shipping.
  3. Winner's payment is checked first (test check only — no real processor yet) and held in escrow. Seller is paid only after delivery + 12 hours.
  4. To ask for a refund you must send BOTH a photo AND a video of the item. Winners get a receipt + video receipt as proof.
  5. Replicas / unbranded items must say so. A replica cannot be claimed as real. Banned items are blocked. Report a listing if something is wrong.
  6. Auctions: bid slider, custom bid, 10s / 30s / 2 min / custom timer. Sudden Death = outbid does not reset the timer. Regular = outbid adds 10 seconds.
  7. Store is Buy Now only — no bidding, no timer. No automatic Buy It Now in auctions; the seller may post a Buy It Now offer in chat.
  8. Founding sellers: first 10, 0% until it goes live, then 5% forever. Normal fee is 10% on the item price only, not shipping.
  9. Live Now means live in chat — no video yet.
