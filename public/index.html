Rules — short version

  1. Owner is not responsible for refunds. Refunds only until the seller is paid out — the refund window closes on the payout date for that sale (12 hours for trusted/top small items, 3 days or 7 days for longer payout holds), for a legit reason only (not as described, damaged, wrong item, defective, never arrived). After the payout date, no refund. "Changed my mind" is not accepted. If a refund is asked, it is up to the seller: refund without the item back, or ask for the item back first — then no refund until the seller receives it, or the return is scanned as delivered. When the return is scanned as DELIVERED, the refund releases right away on its own — the seller does not have to tap anything — and if the seller never confirms receipt after the return was shipped, the refund releases on its own 48 hours after the return was shipped, so the seller cannot stall it forever and the buyer does not lose out. In test mode delivery is recorded from the tracking / mark-as-delivered step — no live carrier feed yet; at go-live a real carrier delivery scan will do this automatically.
  2. Shipping must be insured. Buyers pay shipping — the price is set by package size: Small $4.47, Medium $4.85, Large $5.25, XL $6.75, Box 1–5 lb $7.75, Big box over 5 lb $9.69+, Flat Rate box $12.99, Card envelope $0.99. Carrier is automatic: everything ships via USPS, except Big box over 5 lb, which ships via UPS. No FedEx.
  3. Winner's payment is checked first (test check only — no real processor yet) and held in escrow. Seller is paid after delivery, on their payout tier — see the payout rule below.
  4. How fast sellers get paid. Sellers earn their payout speed with clean sales (a sale that completed, was delivered and paid out, with no refund or dispute). New seller (0–2 clean sales): under $100 is paid 3 days after delivery, $100 and up is paid 7 days after delivery. Trusted seller (3–9 clean sales): under $100 is paid 12 hours after delivery, $100–$500 is paid 3 days after delivery, over $500 is paid 7 days after delivery. Top seller (10+ clean sales and a Seller Scorecard average of 4.0 or more, if they have any scores): everything is paid 12 hours after delivery. The refund window for buyers runs until the payout date — the same rule as the old 12-hour rule, applied to the longer hold: legit reason only, photo + video required, and no refund after the payout date. A payout never comes out while a refund is pending. Payout dates are tracked dates in test mode — no real payment processor is connected yet, so no real bank transfer moves.
  5. To ask for a refund you must send BOTH a photo AND a video of the item — the refund form stays locked until both are attached — and ask before the refund window closes on the payout date. Winners get a receipt + video receipt as proof. A refund comes out of the seller's escrow money, not the owner's fees. Seller's choice on returns: when a refund is asked, the seller decides — refund without the item back (buyer keeps it), or ask for the item back first. If the seller asks for it back, a tracked return label is sent to the buyer, and no refund goes out until the seller confirms they received the item back, or the return is scanned as delivered. When the return is scanned as DELIVERED the refund releases right away — no seller tap needed — and if the seller never confirms, it releases on its own 48 hours after the return was shipped. The return label is paid for by the seller, but only because the seller asked for the item back — it is not the buyer's cost and not the owner's fees. The return label is a test label record (same carrier rule: USPS, except Big box over 5 lb = UPS) — no real carrier connected yet, and delivery is recorded from the tracking / mark-as-delivered step until a real carrier feed is connected at go-live. If the buyer never ships the item back before the refund window closes, the refund expires and no refund is given — but once the return IS shipped, it is never expired at the payout date; it waits for the 48-hour release. Note: a bank chargeback is separate — banks can still do chargebacks for 60–120 days, even after the refund window closes.
  6. Returning an item — proof before you ship it back: before the return can be marked shipped, the buyer must attach Video of the item going into the box + picture of the label on the same box when sent out. The video proves what went in; the label picture proves that same box with that label is the one that was sent. Both are required — the return cannot be marked shipped without them. If the wrong item comes back: once the return is delivered or shipped back, the seller has 48 hours to check what arrived. If the wrong item, an empty box, or a different item arrived, the seller taps "Wrong item sent back" and must attach BOTH a photo AND a video of what actually arrived — no photo + video, no flag. Tapping it pauses the refund right away: the 48-hour auto-release stops, the money stays in escrow, and no refund goes out while the owner reviews it. The owner compares three things side by side — the buyer's return proof, the seller's photo + video of what arrived, and the original receipt + video receipt — and decides: (1) Buyer sent the wrong item — the refund is denied, and the buyer must send back the right item on a new tracked return label (the seller pays for it, only because they asked for the item back) within 7 days, or no refund; (2) Item is correct — the claim is not upheld and the refund releases at once. If the seller never flags it within the 48 hours, the refund auto-releases as usual. Incorrect return claims: if the owner rules the item was correct, that sale does not count as a clean sale, the seller drops a tier, and it is recorded on their Seller Scorecard. Selling privileges are suspended for 7 days after a first incorrect claim, 30 days after a second, and removed after a third — during a suspension the seller's listings are hidden and they cannot list new items. The strike count and the stop-until date are shown on the seller's dashboard and in the owner dashboard.
  7. Replicas / unbranded items must say so. A replica cannot be claimed as real. Banned items are blocked. Report a listing if something is wrong.
  8. Auctions: bid slider, custom bid, 10s / 30s / 2 min / custom timer. Sudden Death = outbid does not reset the timer. Regular = outbid adds 10 seconds.
  9. Store is Buy Now only — no bidding, no timer. No automatic Buy It Now in auctions; the seller may post a Buy It Now offer in chat.
  10. Founding sellers: first 10, 0% until it goes live, then 5% forever. Normal fee is 10% on the item price only, not shipping.
  11. Live Now means live on camera + chat — watch the seller and hear them talk, and type in chat what you want to see.
  12. When someone buys or wins and pays, the seller gets a QR code for that exact sale. The QR names the carrier — USPS, or UPS for a Big box over 5 lb. Scan it to open the sale, then tap Mark as shipped. The QR is a shipment record — not real postage yet.
  13. ⏰ Time Wheel: the seller loads the wheel with up to 100 items and buyers see them all before bidding. The winner pays the winning bid + shipping, the wheel spins and lands on one item — that item is what they get. No extra prize, no extra charge. Nobody buys a spot and nobody pays to spin.
  14. 🎲 Random Items: if a seller doesn't want to put a picture up, they can auction numbered random items — Random Item 1, 2, 3, up to 100 — with no picture. The seller holds each item up live on camera. What the winner gets is what was shown live, and the seller types what it was on the sold item page — the video receipt then shows what they won.
